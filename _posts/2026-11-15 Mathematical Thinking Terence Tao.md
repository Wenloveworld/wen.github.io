##Methods

1.Identify Earth's Orbit: use the location of Mars every 687 days as a fixed point.


2.Group Testing -- to find the optimal group size.
  e.g., 1 patient in 100 people. divide the population into halves and test each of the sub-groups. at most 7 test, we can figure out the patient.


3.幻方magic square: 行、列、对角线之和15.


4.Coin weighting problem & "Compressed Sensing" problem applied in MRI 

5.Different ways of tackling problems: go for a walk to think about the essence, use a pen and paper,  talk with other people

6.You have to dig into the data u got, especially failures.

4.Proactive Deconstruction,zoom out, plan, act
